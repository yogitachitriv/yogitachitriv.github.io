Biography

Dr. Sarah Mitchell is an Associate Professor in the Department of Computer Science at the University of Technology, where she leads the Intelligent Systems Research Group. Her research focuses on developing novel machine learning algorithms for real-world applications, with particular emphasis on interpretable AI and ethical considerations in automated decision-making systems. She has published over 40 peer-reviewed papers in top-tier conferences and journals, and her work has been recognized with several awards including the Best Paper Award at NeurIPS 2023. Dr. Mitchell is passionate about bridging the gap between theoretical advances and practical implementations, and she actively collaborates with industry partners to ensure her research has tangible societal impact.
