About

I am an Associate Professor and Assistant Dean (R&D) in the Department of Electronics and Telecommunication at Yeshwantrao Chavan College of Engineering (YCCE), Nagpur, where I have taught and mentored students since 2006. I earned my Ph.D. in Electronics from RTM Nagpur University in 2017, with a thesis on algorithms for the quantitative analysis and assessment of brain images, supervised by Dr. M. M. Mushrif.

My research spans explainable AI, medical image analysis, and machine learning applications in healthcare — including brain MRI segmentation and tumor detection, liver and cardiac disease classification, and agricultural and engineering applications of ML. I have secured external research funding from CSIR-ASPIRE, AICTE-RPS, DST-FIST, and a collaboration with Harvard University funded by USAID.
